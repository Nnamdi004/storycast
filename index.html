Experience stories your way

Accessibility is built in from the first line of markup, not added afterwards.

Read along

Every story has a full transcript. Select any timestamp to jump to that moment, or download the text.

Captions you control

Video stories include English captions. Audio stories show live captions beneath the player.

Keyboard friendly

Every control can be reached and used without a mouse, with a clear focus outline throughout.
