About & Access

Stories should be open to everyone.

StoryCast is a small collection of audio and video stories, designed so that people can hear, watch or read each one.

Our mission

Good stories travel best when nobody is left out. StoryCast pairs every recording with captions and a complete transcript, so a story reaches people who are deaf or hard of hearing, people on slow or noisy connections, and anyone who simply prefers to read.

The project celebrates ordinary voices from African cities and towns: a bus conductor, a weaver, a market seller. The sample stories here are fictional pieces written to show how the format works.

At a glance

  • Captions on every video
  • Full transcript for every story
  • Works with keyboard, screen reader and touch
  • Built to WCAG 2.1 level AA

Accessibility features

What we built, and why it matters.

Captions and transcripts

Video stories carry a captions track. Audio stories show live captions below the player. Each story page includes a full, time-stamped transcript you can read or download.

Keyboard navigation

A skip link jumps past the header. Every link, button and media control is reachable in a logical order, and the focus outline is always visible.

Readable colour and type

Text meets WCAG AA contrast, information is never shown by colour alone, and text scales with your browser settings and reflows down to a 320px-wide screen.

Screen reader support

Pages use landmarks and a clear heading order. The current transcript line is announced with aria-current, and decorative images are hidden from assistive technology.

Respects your settings

Animation and smooth scrolling are switched off when your device asks for reduced motion. You control playback speed and nothing plays automatically.

Works without JavaScript

Players, captions and transcripts work with scripting off. JavaScript only adds clickable timestamps, live captions for audio and the speed control.

How to engage with a story

  1. Choose how to take it in. Press play to listen or watch, or go straight to the transcript.
  2. Turn on what helps. Use the captions button on videos, the playback speed menu, and the optional follow-along scrolling for the transcript.
  3. Jump around. Select a timestamp in the transcript to play from that line.

Keyboard shortcuts

Moving around StoryCast with a keyboard
KeyWhat it does
Tab / Shift + TabMove forward or back through links, buttons and controls.
Enter or SpaceActivate the focused link or button, including transcript timestamps.
Space on the playerPlay or pause when the media player has focus.
← / → on the playerSkip back or forward a few seconds in most browsers.
↑ / ↓ in the transcriptScroll the transcript once it has focus.

Native player shortcuts vary slightly between browsers.

Accessibility statement

StoryCast aims to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1 at level AA. This site has been tested with keyboard-only navigation and automated contrast checks.

Known limitations. The sample stories use synthetic narration and abstract visuals. Real recordings should be reviewed to confirm captions are accurate, and a separate audio description may be needed for visual content.
